BEFORE YOU BOOK

Booking & Cancellation Policy

We want guests to know exactly what they are booking and what happens if plans change.

Important: The owner must confirm the property's deposit, payment, cancellation and refund rules. Until those rules are approved, this page explains what guests should ask and what will be confirmed in writing; it does not invent a fixed cancellation window or fee.

How to book

  1. Send your requested arrival and departure dates, number of guests, room type and number of rooms.
  2. The guest house will confirm availability, the room offered, the total price and any applicable conditions.
  3. Your booking is not confirmed until you receive written confirmation from the guest house or confirmation through your booking platform.
  4. Before paying, ask for the deposit amount (if any), payment deadline, balance due date, cancellation/refund conditions and check-in instructions.

Changes and cancellations

Contact the guest house as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel a booking. The cancellation deadline, fees and refund eligibility depend on the terms confirmed for that reservation. Keep written records of cancellation requests and any response.

Bookings made through another platform

If you booked through Booking.com or another third-party service, review the cancellation and payment conditions shown for that reservation and use the platform's required process where applicable.

Late arrivals and no-shows

Please tell the guest house your expected arrival time and contact them if you are delayed. Ask in advance how late arrivals and no-shows are handled, as these conditions should be stated in your booking confirmation.

Before you travel

  • Save your written booking confirmation and payment receipt.
  • Confirm the address, arrival time, contact number and any access instructions.
  • Confirm the room's occupancy and facilities if these are important to your stay.
