House Rules

These general guidelines help keep the guest house comfortable and safe for guests, neighbours and staff.

Owner review required: Confirm these rules match the property's actual practices. Add any specific quiet hours, smoking areas, visitor rules or penalties only after the owner approves them.

Respect other guests

  • Please be considerate of other guests and keep noise at a reasonable level, especially at night.
  • Follow staff instructions relating to safety, shared spaces and access to the property.
  • Visitors and gatherings should be discussed with the guest house in advance.

Check-in and check-out

The website currently lists check-in from 16:00 and check-out by 09:00. Confirm the times and late-arrival arrangements before travelling. Do not assume early check-in or late check-out is available unless agreed.

Rooms and facilities

  • Use rooms, appliances and shared facilities responsibly and leave shared areas tidy after use.
  • Report maintenance issues, accidental damage or lost property to staff as soon as possible.
  • Do not remove guest house property from the premises.
  • Confirm kitchen and self-catering arrangements with staff if you are unsure which facilities are available for your room.

Safety and parking

  • Use designated parking areas and keep access routes clear.
  • Take care of your personal belongings and report any safety concern promptly.
  • Do not interfere with safety equipment or access controls.

Smoking, pets and children

Please confirm the property's current smoking, pet and child-supervision rules directly with the guest house before booking. These policies have not been specified on this website and should not be assumed.

Damage or concerns

Please tell staff promptly about damage, incidents or concerns so they can be addressed. Any charges or remedies must be consistent with the terms agreed for the booking and applicable law.
