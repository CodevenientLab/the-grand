YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

This notice explains how The Grand Guest House may use personal information shared when you enquire about accommodation or make a booking.

Owner review required: This draft should be checked against the guest house's actual processes and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including who is responsible for privacy queries and how long records are retained.

Information we may collect

Depending on your enquiry or reservation, information may include your name, phone number, email address, requested dates, guest and room counts, messages about your stay, and booking or payment-related records. Please do not send card details, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information through an enquiry form or WhatsApp.

How information may be used

  • Respond to accommodation enquiries and check availability.
  • Arrange, manage and communicate about a reservation or stay.
  • Handle payments, receipts, guest requests, complaints and refunds where applicable.
  • Meet legal, accounting, safety or record-keeping obligations.
  • Protect the guest house, guests and property, where lawful.

Who information may be shared with

Information may be shared with service providers needed to communicate with you or administer a booking, such as email, telephone, messaging, website-form or booking-platform providers, and where required by law. The guest house should not sell personal information. Third-party services may process information under their own privacy terms.

Website enquiries

If you choose WhatsApp, your message opens in WhatsApp and you must press send to submit it. If you choose email, your email application opens and you must send the message. The website may also use a configured form service if one is enabled. Please check the privacy terms of the service you choose.

Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected or to meet applicable legal requirements, and should be protected with appropriate safeguards. The owner should document actual retention periods, access controls and any service providers used.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information is held about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a concern about its use. Identity verification may be needed before a request can be processed. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Contact about privacy

For a privacy query, contact booking@thegrandguesthouse.co.za or call 079 587 5820. The owner should add the designated Information Officer's contact details if applicable.

Draft prepared October 2026. Update this notice to reflect actual data practices before launch.
