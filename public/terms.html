Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the general conditions for enquiries and stays at The Grand Guest House. Please read them before making a booking.

Owner review required: This is a practical draft for the website, not legal advice. The owner should confirm it reflects the property's actual policies before publishing it as binding terms.

1. Enquiries and reservations

  • An enquiry or availability request is not a confirmed reservation.
  • A booking is confirmed only after the guest house has provided written confirmation of the dates, room type, number of guests, total price and payment requirements.
  • Guests should check that the confirmation details are correct and notify the guest house promptly of any errors.
  • Availability and prices may change until a reservation is confirmed.

2. Rates and payment

  • Any price shown on the website is indicative until confirmed directly for the requested dates and occupancy.
  • The guest house will confirm the total amount, any required deposit, payment method and payment deadline before the guest commits to a reservation.
  • Guests should obtain and retain a receipt or written payment confirmation.
  • Do not send payment to an account or number unless the guest house has confirmed that it is an authorised payment channel.

3. Check-in and check-out

The website currently lists check-in from 16:00 and check-out by 09:00. Confirm these times and any early check-in, late check-out or late-arrival arrangements with the guest house before arrival.

4. Guest details and occupancy

  • Guests should provide accurate contact details, dates and the number of adults and children staying.
  • Room occupancy must be agreed with the guest house in advance. Additional guests or changes to the booking may affect the price or availability.
  • Guests should contact the guest house as soon as possible if their arrival time or plans change.

5. Cancellations, changes and no-shows

The applicable cancellation, amendment, refund and no-show conditions must be provided and agreed at the time the booking is confirmed. Ask for these terms in writing before paying. If a reservation was made through a third-party booking platform, that platform's stated terms may also apply.

6. Guest conduct and property

Guests are expected to respect other guests, staff and the property, follow reasonable safety instructions, and report accidental damage promptly. Further house rules are published in the House Rules.

7. Facilities and information

The guest house will confirm the facilities and room features applicable to the selected room. If an advertised facility is important to your stay, please verify its availability before booking.

8. Liability and concerns

Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude or limit any right or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African law. Please raise concerns with the guest house as soon as possible so they can be addressed.

9. Privacy

Personal information supplied in an enquiry or reservation is handled as described in our Privacy Notice.

10. Contact

Contact The Grand Guest House at 079 587 5820 or booking@thegrandguesthouse.co.za with questions or to request the terms that apply to your booking.
